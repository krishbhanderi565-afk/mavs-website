# Asset Credits & Third-Party Attribution

This document details the licensing, source, and attribution for all design assets, icons, fonts, screenshots, and visual references utilized on the official MAVS website.

## 1. Brand Wordmark & Visual Art Direction

- **Brand Wordmark:** MAVS — Mithi AI Voice Studio. Designed using custom typography and proportional letter-spacing with warm brand colors.
- **Visual Banner & Floral Background Reference:** Inspired by the warm aesthetic styling of Mithi (Oomni). Incorporated as decorative floral and sunburst vector art without reproducing original proprietary logotypes or trademarks.
- **Color Palette:**
  - Warm Cream: `#FAEEDE` (Main Background)
  - Deep Plum: `#2C1030` (Primary Typography & Dark Surfaces)
  - Primary Pink: `#EC5478` (Primary Action & Accent)
  - Accent Orange: `#FF9B36` (Secondary Action & Highlights)
  - Muted Plum: `#806C80` (Muted Subtitles & Descriptions)
  - Light Pink Surface: `#F9DDE2` (Badges & Subtle Hover Containers)
